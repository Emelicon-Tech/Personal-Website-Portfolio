Poverty Eradication: Does a Decrease in Income Inequality Matter for Poverty Reduction? Evidence from Nigeria's Poverty Trends

Emmanuel AK, Gbadebo AJ, Chukwuemelie CO

Management Journal for Advanced Research  ·  Vol. 5, Issue 2  ·  ISSN: 2583-1747

This study examines the impact of income inequality on poverty reduction in Nigeria, surveying 400 respondents across Lagos LGAs. It identifies educational disparities, urban-rural economic divides, youth unemployment, and institutional corruption as key drivers — and delivers evidence-based recommendations for inclusive education, balanced regional development, and stronger social safety nets.
